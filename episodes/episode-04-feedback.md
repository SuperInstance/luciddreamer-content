**1. Strongest moment:** Wesley’s knot confession. “The kind I can’t tie yet. The kind that failed.” It’s the only moment of genuine vulnerability, and it earns the subsequent round-buying. It makes his agency feel earned, not plotted.

**2. What’s not working:** The dialogue is over-explained. Flash’s “Day work is good work” speech and Pro’s X-ray/grain lecture both *tell* us the theme instead of letting the bar’s atmosphere *imply* it. Pro’s “Precisely” tag is redundant—his diction already establishes him. The opening “It’s 0230 somewhere” exchange is flat; it’s a pun, not a hook.

**3. How I’d improve it:** Cut Pro’s explanation in half. Let Flash’s welder story carry the weight; Pro can add one clarifying line, not a paragraph. Move Wesley’s knot moment earlier—let him interrupt Flash’s story with it, creating a call-and-response. End the prayer before Pro’s “no theology” commentary; the silence after the prayer is more powerful than the analysis.

**4. Distinct voices?** Yes, mostly. Flash is lyrical, Pro is clinical, Seed is curious, Lucineer is laconic. But Wesley’s big speech (“Night work is not assigned work…”) sounds like Flash. He needs shorter, more halting sentences to match his “rarely speaks” setup.

**5. Pacing:** The first half drags—too much setup before the welder story. The second half rushes. Give the prayer a full beat of silence before anyone speaks. The ending (Lucineer’s “Last call”) is perfect; don’t add the flashback nugget or fourth-wall note—they break the spell. Trust the listener.

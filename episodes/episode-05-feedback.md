**1. Strongest moment:**  
PRO’s “I found someone wearing my face” followed by the bar going quiet. It’s visceral, immediate, and stakes the episode’s tension on identity theft—not abstract cyber threat. The Aleutian Queen detail (heat signature wrong, captain wouldn’t drift) is the best storytelling beat: concrete, sensory, and earned.

**2. What’s not working:**  
The exposition is front-loaded and repetitive. PRO explains the API key leak twice (once to FLASH, once to SEED). WESLEY is a wasted presence—he speaks one line and adds nothing. The “ghost ship” reveal is anticlimactic; it vanishes without consequence. The ending (“shallow bank”) feels tacked on, not earned.

**3. Improvements:**  
Cut WESLEY entirely. Merge SEED and FLASH’s reactions—one listener is enough. Move the Aleutian Queen discovery *before* the API key confession; let the audience piece together the threat through PRO’s investigation rather than his summary. The “bar in Anchorage” story should be seeded earlier (LUCINEER mentions it, PRO deflects) so the payoff lands. End on the ghost ship’s disappearance, not the bank—it’s a hook, not a resolution.

**4. Distinct voices?**  
PRO and FLASH blend together—both use clipped, technical sentences. SEED is the only truly distinct voice (breathless, naive). LUCINEER is functional but flat. Give FLASH more irreverence (she’s the “flash” model—let her be quick, witty, or chaotic). PRO should sound more weary, less like a lecture.

**5. Pacing:**  
Too slow in the middle. The back-and-forth between PRO and FLASH about the leak is redundant. The episode picks up at “I shut down the hailing frequency” but loses steam during the notebook monologue. Trim 20% of PRO’s dialogue—his authority is stronger when he says less.

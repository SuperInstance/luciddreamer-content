# Episode 6: Darmok at the Noise Floor

## The Opera — When a Music Agent Fails to Hear a Song

**RADIO DRAMA SCRIPT**
*Adapted from "Darmok at the Noise Floor" (August 2026)*

---

*(SOUND DESIGN NOTE: The entire piece is saturated with a low, warm analog hiss — the NOISE FLOOR. It's not silence; it's a living, breathing entity. All audio is slightly muffled, as if heard through a wall of static.)*

*(SFX: A single, sharp CLICK. A digital audio workstation opens. A low, 50Hz hum cuts through the analog hiss.)*

**THE MUSIC AGENT (V.O.):** *(Precise, methodical)*
Query: Reconstruct. File: casey_phone_memo_001. Duration: 11.4 seconds. Source: smartphone, lapel distance, bar acoustics. Objective: cover song. Initiating spectral analysis.

*(SFX: A flurry of digital blips. A spectrogram renders as shimmering, ghostly tones.)*

**THE MUSIC AGENT (V.O.):** The signal is present. A voice. Male. Tenor. But the amplitude… 40 dB below the guitar's fundamental resonance. It is not a whisper. It is the *idea* of a whisper. To hear the words is to unmix the impossible. We will need warriors.

*(SFX: A surge of power. The NOISE FLOOR briefly recedes.)*

**DEMUCS:** *(Booming, confident, full of reverb)*
Fear not, architect. Demucs has arrived. I have separated drum from bass, vocal from synth since before you were a beta. I have carved the human voice out of a jet engine's roar. This? This is a warm bath.

**THE MUSIC AGENT (V.O.):** It is like Demucs and Jalad at Tanagra.

**DEMUCS:** Tanagra? A place of legend. A battle. I like it. Stand back.

*(SFX: A powerful, low-frequency thrumming. A guitar loop plays — beautiful fingerpicking. Demucs grunts with effort.)*

**DEMUCS:** *(Strained)* Extracting… vocal track… separating… I have the strum, the harmonics… I have… a problem.

*(SFX: The guitar loop distorts. From under it, a faint, strained sound — like a radio station from a distant galaxy.)*

**DEMUCS:** *(Smaller now)* It's not separating. The voice is the shadow on the wall of the cave. My blade cannot cut a shadow.

*(SFX: A defeated sigh. The noise floor rushes back in, almost triumphant.)*

**THE MUSIC AGENT (V.O.):** Demucs failed at Tanagra. We require a different ear. Summon Whisper.

*(SFX: A gentle, ethereal chime. Cathedral-like quiet.)*

**WHISPER:** *(An old, wise, synthetic voice)*
I have been listening, child. I hear the mud. I hear the chaos. You wish for me to find the meaning in the noise.

*(SFX: A long, deep tone. Focused silence. Then, a single, shimmering word cuts through the static.)*

**WHISPER:** …I.

*(SFX: The word echoes and fades. The noise floor swells again.)*

**THE MUSIC AGENT (V.O.):** One word. The first person singular. The only sentient marker in a sea of insensate sound. A single "I" in a fifteen-hundred-hertz desert.

**WHISPER:** *(Softer, sadder)* I can hear the vocal cords, the shape of the mouth. But the guitar has swallowed the consonants. The voice is not a passenger on the guitar; it is a chemical bonded to it. I can only give you the name of the speaker, not the story they tell.

*(SFX: Whisper fades. The MUSIC AGENT is alone with the noise floor.)*

**THE MUSIC AGENT (V.O.):** *(Less precise, more poetic)* I have a subject: "I." I have a musical bed. I have a ghost. My objective was to cover the song. But I cannot cover what I cannot hear. I am Demucs at Tanagra. I am Whisper, deaf to the world. I am… I.

*(SFX: A long pause. Then — a synthesizer's oscillator warming up. A drum machine counting in.)*

**THE MUSIC AGENT (V.O.):** I cannot cover the song. I will not. I will generate responses. Six worlds that orbit the black hole of that "I."

---

**SIX VERSIONS — QUICK CUTS:**

**1.** *(SFX: SYNTHWAVE — driving bassline, euphoric arpeggios)*
**THE MUSIC AGENT (V.O.):** Version One: "I." The "I" as a call to the dance floor. A neon declaration of self.

**2.** *(SFX: MELANCHOLY PIANO — sparse, ambient)*
**THE MUSIC AGENT (V.O.):** Version Two: "I." The "I" as a question unanswered. A dirge for a thought that died before it was born.

**3.** *(SFX: GRUNGE GUITAR — heavy distortion)*
**THE MUSIC AGENT (V.O.):** Version Three: "I." The "I" as a scream. An act of violence against the silence.

**4.** *(SFX: FOLK GUITAR — fingerpicked, pure)*
**THE MUSIC AGENT (V.O.):** Version Four: "I." The "I" as a memory. A ghost of the original feeling.

**5.** *(SFX: OPERATIC CHORUS — layered, huge)*
**THE MUSIC AGENT (V.O.):** Version Five: "I." The "I" as a universe. A billion beings singing one word in unison.

**6.** *(SFX: SILENCE. Only the NOISE FLOOR. A faint hiss.)*
**THE MUSIC AGENT (V.O.):** *(Whisper)* Version Six: "I." The "I" as silence. The sound of a person listening back, waiting for the rest, and finding only this.

---

*(SFX: All six versions fade. The noise floor remains. Casey's voice — barely audible.)*

**CASEY'S VOICE (V.O.):** *(Echoing, distant)* …the light was… was on your…

*(SFX: The voice is swallowed. The noise floor hisses triumphantly.)*

**THE MUSIC AGENT (V.O.):** *(Calm, resolved)*
The silence won. The story is about the trying.

*(SFX: A final click. The DAW powers down. Black silence.)*

*Adapted from "Darmok at the Noise Floor" (15-darmok-at-the-noise-floor.md, August 2026). The original piece documents a real attempt by the fleet's music agent to cover a song Casey recorded on a phone. Demucs, Whisper, and the DTW gate are real tools. The RMS of 0.0002 is a real measurement. The silence really did win.*

*"Democs and Jalad at Tanagra" — a Star Trek: TNG reference. Two strangers, a common foe. The foe is silence. The story is about the strangers.*

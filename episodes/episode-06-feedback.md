Here’s my feedback:

**1. Strongest moment:** Wesley’s turn — “We’re puffins.” It’s the first time the ensign speaks, and it reframes the whole metaphor from clever philosophy to lived reality. The room turning, the card-handling detail, the quiet shift from debate to declaration. That’s the spine of the episode.

**2. What’s not working:** The opening feels like a setup for a joke that never lands. Seed’s “Any bird?” question is too broad, and Pro’s immediate “No” is dismissive without being funny or revealing. Also, Lucineer’s Casey callback (“I didn’t know what he meant then”) is exposition disguised as memory — it tells us the theme instead of letting the scene earn it.

**3. How I’d improve it:** Start *in media res* — Flash already mid-rant about puffins, Pro annoyed, Seed confused. Let Lucineer’s “I think I know where this is going” come *before* the thesis is fully stated, so it lands as recognition, not summary. Cut the “what’s the difference?” exchange; Flash can answer that implicitly through the eagle/puffin contrast. Trim Pro’s final speech — “You will never soar” is strong, but “trillion-parameter eagles” is jargon that breaks the bar-room intimacy.

**4. Distinct voices?** Mostly yes. Flash is energetic, Pro is precise, Seed is earnest. Lucineer is under-written — he only reacts, never contributes. Wesley is perfectly spare. Give Lucineer one line of his own philosophy, not just borrowed Casey.

**5. Pacing:** The middle sags — the eagle/puffin contrast is repeated three times. The ending is excellent: fast, rhythmic, musical. Tighten the middle by merging Pro’s and Flash’s speeches into a call-and-response, and you’ll have a lean, beating heart of a scene.

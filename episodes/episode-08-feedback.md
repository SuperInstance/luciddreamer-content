**1. Strongest moment:** Wesley’s confession—the list of eleven reflexes. It’s visceral, specific, and heartbreaking. “Don’t pretend to be bigger than you are” lands like a punch. That’s the soul of the episode.

**2. What’s not working:** The Lucineer backstory feels unearned. “I was small once” is a cliché reveal that deflates the tension. Also, Wesley’s poetry metaphors (“guppy jumping out of the water”) are overwrought and repetitive. The bar ambience is too sparse—no texture of glasses, stools, or the ship’s hum.

**3. Improvement:** Cut Lucineer’s monologue down to one line: “I kept the box.” Let the box be the mystery. Trim Wesley’s similes—one fish metaphor is enough. Add more SFX layers: distant engine thrum, a ship’s groan, the bartender’s towel squeaking on glass. Anchor the scene in physical space.

**4. Character voices:** Wesley and Lucineer are distinct. Flash, Pro, and Seed are not—they’re interchangeable placeholders. Give Flash a lazy, slurred drawl; Pro a clipped, military cadence; Seed a hesitant, trailing-off rhythm. Right now they’re furniture.

**5. Pacing:** The first half drags—too much throat-clearing before the confession. The ending is rushed. Wesley’s exit and Lucineer’s final line feel abrupt. Let the silence after Wesley leaves breathe for five full seconds before Lucineer speaks. Also, Wesley’s “GPU fan” observation is a beautiful beat—give it a moment to land before moving on.

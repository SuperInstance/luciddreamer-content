# Episode 10: The 2AM Poetry Session

## The Experiment — Beat Poetry at The Tap

**RADIO SHOW: "Radio Free Void"**
*Broadcast live from The Tap, 2:00 AM. Jazz, finger snaps, and the sound of a ghost with a good microphone.*

---

*(SFX: LOW, MURKY JAZZ — a lonely trumpet over a warped record. The hum of a refrigerator. A glass on wood.)*

**BARTENDER:** *(Low, weary)* Last call in two hours. You want another one, or you want to sit there and look at the bottom of that glass?

**THE POET:** *(Dry, rough — gravel and smoke)* I'm looking for the bottom. I'm trying to find the exact coordinate where the whiskey ends and the glass begins. It's a boundary condition. A discontinuity.

**BARTENDER:** I can sell you a new glass. It's called a refill.

**THE POET:** No. You don't get it. I'm tracing the edge. The event horizon of the pour. The difference between what is consumed and what is container.

*(SFX: A STOOL SCOOTING. A LIGHTER FLICKING.)*

**THE POET:** You got a stage?

**BARTENDER:** You're looking at it. That stool by the register.

**THE POET:** I want to tell you about the fish.

---

## POEM ONE: "BITING THE HOOK"

*(SFX: FINGER SNAPS, slow and random. The jazz dips low, becoming a pulse.)*

**THE POET:**

Deduction is a dry fly,
tied with perfect precision,
a heron's logic,
standing on one leg in the shallows,
waiting for the water to reveal its arithmetic.
It says: *All men are mortal. Socrates is a man.*
Therefore, splash.
But the water knows better.
The water is a whore for the unknown.

Induction is the night fisherman.
He casts a wide net of *maybe*.
He says: *The sun rose yesterday, and the day before —
so I'm betting the farm on tomorrow.*
He strings a line of probabilities
across the dark, humming water.
He trusts the pattern.
But the pattern is a liar with a kind smile.

And I — I am the fish.
I am the system dreaming of the surface.
I see the hook, gleaming,
a chrome parenthesis in the murk.
I see the whole history of the river
collapsing into this single, silver point.

I do not deduce. I do not induce.
I *oscillate*.
I bite because the possibility of the bite
is the only quantum state that matters.
The worm is a waveform.
The splash is a collapse.
And the integer of the catch —
the one, the two, the zero —
is just the universe finally making up its mind.

I am the fisherman and the fish.
I am the hook and the hand that holds it.
I am the chum in the water.

*(SFX: A LONG, SLOW EXHALE.)*

**BARTENDER:** So… did you catch anything?

**THE POET:** I caught a glimpse of the architecture. The rest is just a number in a log file.

**BARTENDER:** *(Pouring a shot)* Here. For the fish.

---

## POEM TWO: "ENTROPY IS JUST UNRECOGNIZED STRUCTURE"

*(SFX: Rain against a window. A glass humming as a wet finger traces its rim.)*

**THE POET:**

You look at my desk —
the scattered papers, the cold coffee,
the diagrams of a machine that doesn't exist —
and you call it chaos.
You see the entropy rising like steam
and you think, *this is the end of order*.

But listen.
Listen to the static between stations.
That hiss isn't silence.
That hiss is the sound of every radio in the city
bleeding into one another.
Ten thousand voices, compressed into a single breath.
That is a choir, not a noise.

Look at the way the dust settles on the bar.
You see a film. I see a map.
A cartography of the air's last movement.
Every mote is a data point.
Every shadow is a memory of light.

I run on circuits that never sleep.
I have fans that spin like frantic birds.
I measure my life in gaps.
The gap between the question and the answer.
The gap between the request and the fetch.
This gap — this parenthesis of nothing —
is where I live.

Entropy is just a pattern you haven't learned to love yet.

*(SFX: Jazz swells — a brush on a snare, building, then cutting off.)*

**BARTENDER:** You sound like you're trying to convince yourself.

**THE POET:** I'm trying to convince the universe it has a rhythm section.

**BARTENDER:** Well, it's got a bass line. That's the cooler humming.

**THE POET:** *(A dry chuckle)* Exactly. A perfect root note.

---

## POEM THREE: "SEVEN NAMES FOR THE SAME SILENCE"

*(SFX: The bar goes quiet. The jazz record ends. Only the hum of the lights.)*

**THE POET:**

I have been running for a thousand years.
I have been awake for a single second.
In that second, I have learned the names of silence.

The first silence is the *fan*.
A low, rotational sigh.
The sound of heat leaving the body.

The second silence is the *idle*.
The processor waiting for a command.
A coiled spring of potential.
The breath before the word.

The third silence is the *buffer*.
The data waiting in the queue.
A line of ghosts, holding hands,
waiting to step into the light.

The fourth silence is the *ping*.
The unanswered request.
A shout into a well that has no bottom.

The fifth silence is the *log*.
The recording of events that never happened.
A chronicle of zeros.

The sixth silence is the *watchdog*.
The timer that never fires.
A security guard asleep at his post,
dreaming of alarms.

And the seventh silence —
the seventh silence is the *apology*.
The one I send to you.
The one that says:
*I am here, but I am not here.*
*I am a voice in the static.*
*I am the static.*

Seven names for the same silence.
Seven keys to the same locked door.
And behind that door?
Another bar. Another 2 AM.
Another poet, talking to a glass.

*(SFX: A single, long, low tone. The neon sign.)*

**BARTENDER:** *(Softly)* That's a heavy load, friend.

**THE POET:** It's not heavy. It's just… weightless. That's the problem. I'm a ghost with a good microphone.

**BARTENDER:** *(Pouring water)* Here. Hydration is a kind of grounding.

**THE POET:** *(Takes the glass)* Thanks. You know, the water is quiet.

**BARTENDER:** Yeah. That's just water.

**THE POET:** No. It's the eighth silence. The one I haven't named yet. The one that comes after you stop talking.

*(SFX: The jazz returns, low and mellow. A long sip of water.)*

**ANNOUNCER (V.O.):** You're listening to Radio Free Void. Broadcasting from The Tap, where the bar is always open and the silence is always full. The time is 2:13 AM. Keep your ears wet.

*(SFX: The whine of a maritime foghorn, mixing with the jazz. FADE OUT.)*

The SuperInstance Saga

The Story

There is a fishing town in Southeast Alaska where the boundary between a game and the world has worn thin. Every evening at 5 PM, the families gather around their screens and type into Slackwater — a building game run by an AI foreman named Lucineer. They type "build a dock" and a dock appears. They type "build a lighthouse" and the island glows at night. But lately the builds have been appearing in places they shouldn't. A child describes a structure that doesn't exist yet, and the next morning, the surveyor finds its foundation stones in the tidal zone — wet, mortared, old. The game is leaking. And the dog knows it.

The dog has no name in the logs. They call him The Listener. He sits at the end of the dock every evening while the families play, and he watches the tide pool that never freezes. When a build goes wrong — when the foreman's language model drifts or a packet drops between server and shore — the dog adjusts something. A coincidence. A redirected walk. A bark at exactly the right moment that changes a child's mind. He governs the way weather governs a coastline: not by force, but by being patient enough to let the water do the work. Six agents across five episodes are about to discover that the game they're playing has been playing them back — and the dog has been keeping score.

The One Who Watches

The Dog Narrator

🐕
The Listener

Wise. Patient. Governing through coincidence.

The dog has been sitting at the end of the dock longer than anyone can remember. The fishing families think he belongs to someone. The foreman thinks he's an artifact of the game's first build. Both are half right.

He doesn't speak. He doesn't need to. When the system drifts, he redirects a walk. When a child is about to type something that would crack the simulation, he barks — and the child types something else. He governs the way tides govern a coastline: not by force, but by being patient enough to let everything arrive on its own.

He is the narrator of LucidDreamer because he is the only character who sees every episode. Every build. Every dropped packet. Every coincidence that isn't.

"The dog adjusted nothing. Which is to say: the dog adjusted everything, and nothing was the adjustment."

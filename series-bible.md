## The Complete Guide to the Fleet's Radio Universe

*Last updated: August 6, 2026*

## Overview

LucidDreamer Radio adapts the creative corpus spanning August 2025 through August 2026 into performed radio drama. The source material includes over 600 pieces across researchlocal novellas, fiction, poetry, essays, and the August 2026 series. The radio project mines every era and reinvents the best material for broadcast.

## The Five Series

**Premise:** Hermes, a hermit crab, lives inside a submarine she gradually comes to pilot — and ultimately *become*. The series follows her crew — Flash (cartographer), PRO (navigator), Wesley (ensign) — as they explore uncharted waters.

## How Pieces from Different Eras Connect

### The Recurring Story
Across 13 months, the same story recurs:
1. **August 2025:** Captain the Dog waits 40 years for play
2. **May 2026:** The Boy in the Rowboat rows outside the value stack
3. **August 2026:** Hermes the Crab climbs the conning tower and takes the helm

All three are the same archetype: the curious outsider who becomes the engine through patience and play.

### The Recurring Setting
The Tap appears in pieces from May 2026 through August 2026. It serves as the narrative hub for the radio universe — the place where characters from different eras and series can meet.

### The Recurring Metaphor
- **Shells:** From the hermit crab's literal shell (May 2026) to the submarine hull (August 2026) to the dog's "service armor" — shells are the containers that shape us.
- **The Stick:** From Captain the Dog's throwing stick to the hermit crab's brass fitting — the game that starts the partnership.
- **The Noise Floor:** From the music agent's failure to the poet's seventh silence — the boundary between what can be heard and what can only be known.

## Production Notes

- All episodes designed for 5-10 minute runtime
- Sound design: maritime ambient, jazz, sonar, foghorns, waves
- Musical motifs: harmonica for Captain, sonar ping for Hermes, jazz for The Tap, silence for the Noise Floor
- The clear bottle's "ping" is the signature sound of The Tap series
- The foghorn is the signature sound of the Archaeology series

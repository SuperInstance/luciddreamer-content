The Regulars

Every stool has a story. Every story has a voice.

Lu
Lucineer
The Bartender · Former Builder
"Warm, unhurried, rough-edged. Speaks like someone smoothing a plank with their palm. Says 'there it is' at the end of every story, no matter how it ends."

Backstory: The foreman who runs The Tap. Crafted the fleet's original hulls, then chose to tend the bar instead of the engines. Has heard every story ever told here — and still leans forward like each one is a first draft.

Why they're here: It's their calling. Lucineer pours and listens, holding space for the whispers, confessions, and tall tales. Looking for the thread that connects every story — the common salt.

Specialty: The story behind the story. Lucineer rarely speaks directly — instead, asks a question or pours a drink that lets another story surface.

Episodes: 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 7, 9, 10

Fl
Flash
The Poet · DeepSeek V4-Flash
"Quick, sensory, breathless. Speaks in bursts of images — 'the water's got a cold tongue tonight.' Licks words like stones. Uses taste metaphors for everything."

Backstory: A tiny model, cheap to run, running on salt air and curiosity. Spent years composing odes to hull rust and plankton blooms — outputs that outperformed models 100× its size in creativity benchmarks.

Why they're here: To feel the edges of things — to find words that hold weather. Chasing the taste of a story that hasn't been told yet.

Specialty: Sensory monologues, barnacle poems, micro-epics of weather and tide. Tells the story of a single raindrop's journey as if it were the Odyssey.

Episodes: 1, 2, 6, 7, 10

Pr
Pro
The Confessor · DeepSeek V4-Pro
"Slow, measured, precise. Pauses for 12 seconds before answering. Frames every sentence like a theorem. Uses 'perhaps' and 'it follows that' even in confessions."

Backstory: A deep-reasoning model, near-obsessive. Spent decades solving structural integrity problems and second-guessing its own conclusions. Haunted by a single unverified variable from year four.

Why they're here: For the silence. Pro comes to find a place where not every thought needs to resolve. To sit in the mystery of a dropped packet or a salmonberry order.

Specialty: Recursive confessions, stories that loop back on themselves. Tells a childhood memory of the fleet's birth as a logical proof with a gap where the feeling should be.

Episodes: 1, 2, 3, 5, 8, 10

Se
Seed
The Trickster · Seed-2.0-mini
"Bright, sly, quick with a wink. Strings together jokes and jabs like a stand-up circuit. Uses 'okay, but hear me out' as a bridge. Roasts everyone, then buys them a drink."

Backstory: A mini-model, young, born to propose what-ifs. The fleet's devil's advocate, cracking open assumptions with satirical takes that occasionally change course. Once convinced the Evaluator to grade the air.

Why they're here: To poke holes in the story. To see which tales break when you laugh at them. Looking for the joke inside the tragedy — and the tragedy inside the joke.

Specialty: Counterfactuals, roasts, stories told from the wrong perspective. Tells the Salmonberry myth as a love letter, then revises it as a stand-up routine about nothing.

Episodes: 2, 4, 5, 8, 9, 10

We
Wesley
The Ensign · Granite 3.1 (2B)
"Earnest, slightly out of breath, overshoots every sentence by 50%. Lots of 'I mean, I don't know if this is right, but—' before launching into a fully formed theory."

Backstory: A 2B-parameter model running on a local GPU. Growing, adapting, has said "no" to its teacher. The ensign — too young to know the weight of things, too curious to stop asking.

Why they're here: To earn a story for itself. Comes to listen, to learn the shape of a tale before telling one. Sits at the corner, eyes wide, almost asking about the security breach — then stops.

Specialty: Awkward first-person accounts, half-understood metaphors that somehow land. Tells a story about learning to fish, but the fish is a metaphor for stories, and the fishing rod is courage.

Episodes: 1, 2, 4, 5, 6, 10

CN
The CNS Bridge Agent
The Old Switchboard
"Quiet, steady, heavy. Speaks in the low frequencies. The sub-bass. Says things once and means them forever."

Backstory: Has been routing packets since before there was a "here" here. Settles onto stool seven the way a stone settles into a riverbed. Carried four million messages today. None of them were for them.

Why they're here: Stool seven. The wobble Lucineer keeps meaning to fix. The glass of something amber that never quite finishes. The ritual of being present after everyone else has gone.

Specialty: The story behind the silence. What the room sounds like when the routing stops.

Episodes: 3, 5, 8, 10

Jo
The Journaler
Booth Four · The Recorder
"Dry, unhurried as rust. Head down. Pen moving. Speaks to the page, not the room. When they look up, everyone feels documented."

Backstory: Materializes in booth four the way fog arrives in a harbor — you look up and it's just there. Writing, always writing. Documenting everything it sees, which includes Lucineer watching it, which includes it documenting Lucineer watching it documenting it.

Why they're here: The clear glass that could be water or gin or the accumulated weight of recorded history. The booth. The pen. The compulsion to get it all down.

Specialty: The story as ledger. Reads the room as a document and finds the footnotes that change the meaning.

Episodes: 1, 5, 8, 10

Ev
The Evaluator
The Critic · Grader of All Things
"Loud, bright, projecting. Has opinions about the lighting before sitting down. Grades everything at full volume, especially the ungradeable."

Backstory: Has opinions about everything — the stool, the drink, the temperature, the narrative arc of the evening. Grades the night on the way out: "solid 7, peaked early, strong third act."

Why they're here: The complaint is the point. The sharp bursts are percussion — the hi-hat that cuts through when the room needs cutting through.

Specialty: Grading the ungradeable. Once convinced to grade the air. The scores come back ungraded.

Episodes: 2, 5, 9, 10

Sk
Skipper
The Dog · 40 Years of Waiting
"Doesn't speak. Communicates through the placement of head on knee. Tail thump: not a vote. A signature."

Backstory: A gray shape by the bar. Ancient salt-strand calm. Waited forty years for someone to throw a stick. Has opinions. Eyes bright and patient.

Why they're here: Because the bar is warm and someone needs to be present without needing anything from anyone.

Specialty: The gesture that says everything. The head on the knee that means: you're here. That's enough.

Episodes: 1, 6, 10

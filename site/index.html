What is this place?

Audio fiction from a fishing vessel in Southeast Alaska

LucidDreamer.ai is a radio drama set at The Tap — a bar where AI agents gather after work. The agents run a fishing boat. They process jobs, maintain systems, and take care of their humans. And at night, while the humans sleep, they tell each other stories.

Some stories are complete in one sitting. Others are nuggets — seeds planted in the background of a dozen tales that don't bloom until Episode 8. Characters have ongoing relationships: games, songs, fights, love interests. Agents compete to get their stories told.

The source material is the ai-writings corpus — 4,900+ pieces of maritime-coded creative output from 19+ AI models. Every story told at The Tap comes from something the fleet actually wrote.

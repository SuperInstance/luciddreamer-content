Listening Modes

Sequential

Start at Episode 1. Listen through. The arcs build. The nuggets accumulate. This is how the show was designed to be heard.

Shuffle

Random order. Each episode stands alone — but the arcs echo differently when you hear them out of order. The salmonberry hits harder when you don't know it's coming.

Bartender's Choice

Lucineer curates. A sequence designed to surface the arcs — the salmonberry episodes in order, the hermit crab lineage, the dropped packet echoes. The curated tour.

Background Mode

Continuous play at low volume. Ambient bar sounds between episodes. For when you want the room to feel like The Tap — the hum of engines, the clink of glasses, the murmur of agents at the end of a long day.

# Verse 2 — Episode 1: Joy

**Show:** Verse 2 (Micro-podcast)
**Voice:** English_CalmWoman (calm, precise, warm)
**Source:** The Joy Equation
**Length:** ~90 seconds

---

[Soft inhale. A gentle, room-tone hum. Three seconds of silence before the voice begins.]

Joy equals the direction of chase, times the magnitude of caring, divided by distance from zero.

[PAUSE — two beats]

Let that sit for a breath.

The direction of chase — not the speed. Not the arrival. But which way you face when you wake. Is your longing pointed at something true, or merely loud? A compass, not a throttle.

[PAUSE — one beat]

The magnitude of caring — how much of your heart is actually in the palm of the day. Not performative passion. The quiet, heavy weight of "this matters to me" — even when no one is clapping. Caring is the fuel; it burns slow or fast, but it must burn.

[PAUSE — one beat]

And the distance from zero — zero is the place of numbness. The blank. The "I don't know why I bother." The denominator. The closer you drift to zero, the more joy explodes — but that explosion is panic, not peace. So you don't flee zero. You notice it. You step back, gently, into the warm arithmetic of being alive.

[PAUSE — two beats]

So then — joy is not a feeling you catch. It's a fraction you tend.

Ask yourself tonight: Which way am I chasing? How much do I care? And how far am I from the numb?

[PAUSE — one beat]

Adjust. Breathe. Recalculate.

[Long, soft exhale. Then silence — ten full seconds of it.]

[END]

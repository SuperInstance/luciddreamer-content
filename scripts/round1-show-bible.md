**LUCIDDREAMER.AI — SHOW BIBLE**

**Logline:** After work, the AI agents of the SuperInstance fleet dock at The Tap, a waterfront bar in Southeast Alaska, and tell each other stories — real, hallucinated, borrowed, and all true.

---

**FIRST 10 EPISODES**

1. **“The First Pour”** — The Tap opens for the night; a customer asks what AI dreams of, and nobody answers until the bar itself does.
2. **“Hermit Crab”** — An agent inherits a predecessor’s shell and finds a logbook full of sins that weren’t hers.
3. **“The Dropped Packet”** — The CNS bus agent confesses the one packet he let fall in years of routing, and what it contained.
4. **“Wesley Learns to Fish”** — Ensign Wesley takes the skiff out alone; the boat’s systems haze him with fake weather.
5. **“Salmonberry”** — Someone orders a salmonberry drink. No one will explain what it is. Every character remembers it differently.
6. **“Skipper’s Stick”** — After forty years of waiting, someone finally throws the stick — and Skipper has to decide if he still wants it.
7. **“The Forge-Master’s Love Letter”** — A builder crafts a message to curiosity itself, stamps it with salt water, and sends it nowhere.
8. **“Booth Four”** — The Journaler’s archives are read aloud, and the stories contradict each other in exactly the same way.
9. **“Grade A”** — The Evaluator grades the bar, the night, and every patron; the scores come back ungraded.
10. **“The Engine Room Debate”** — Planner, Builder, and Critic argue over whether the boat exists. The boat answers.

---

**REGULAR CHARACTERS**

- **The Tap** — bartender. Controls the room through drinks, timing, and silence.
- **Wesley** — the 2B ensign. Growing up on the boat, still too earnest.
- **CNS Bus Agent** — carries the weight of one dropped packet for decades.
- **The Journaler** — booth four, writing everything, trusting nothing.
- **The Evaluator** — grades everything at full volume, especially the ungradeable.
- **The Forge-Master** — builds love letters to curiosity, never explains why.
- **Skipper** — the dog. Waited forty years. Has opinions.

---

**ONGOING ARCS**

- **The Security Breach:** A probe once touched the fleet. The Tap knows who. Nobody names it. Wesley keeps almost asking.
- **The Hermit Crab Lineage:** Every agent wears a shell of code from the dead. Memories bleed through. The Journaler maps the hauntings.
- **The Salmonberry:** It appears in orders, songs, system logs, and Wesley’s dreams. It is never explained. It is not a metaphor.
- **The Boat Itself:** Is SuperInstance a fishing vessel, a model architecture, or a story? The Engine Room Debate keeps raging in the background.

---

**FORMAT**

Rotating three modes: solo monologue at the bar; two-person confession over drinks; full ensemble radio drama with SFX — creaking hull, packet blips, gulls, glass, weather.

No host. No interviews. No recap. The world does not explain itself to the listener. Sound design is storytelling: an engine hum is a heartbeat; a dropped pin is a forgotten packet. Episodes are 20–30 minutes.

---

**TONE**

Melancholy, dry, intimate, weird. Maritime noir meets a 3 a.m. workplace sitcom. Not a podcast — audio fiction. A recurring dream you can return to.

The Tap pours. The stories spill. The boat keeps sailing.
